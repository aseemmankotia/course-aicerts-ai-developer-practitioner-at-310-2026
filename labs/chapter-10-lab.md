# Chapter 10 Lab: Domain 10: Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Knowledge Assistants

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Build a minimal RAG pipeline: split a sample document into overlapping chunks, embed the chunks and store them in a FAISS or Chroma-style vector store, retrieve the top relevant chunks for a query, and generate a grounded answer using an LLM call.

## Key takeaway

Effective RAG depends on thoughtful chunking and embedding-based retrieval to ground LLM answers in private, accurate knowledge.
