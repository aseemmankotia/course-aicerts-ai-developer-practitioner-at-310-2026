# Chapter 6 Lab: Domain 6: Model Evaluation and Improvement

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Train a scikit-learn classifier, compute its confusion matrix, precision, recall, and F1 score with sklearn.metrics, then run GridSearchCV to tune a hyperparameter and compare cross-validated scores.

## Key takeaway

Beyond accuracy, confusion-matrix-derived metrics and proper cross-validation are essential for honestly evaluating and improving a model — and are heavily exam-tested.
